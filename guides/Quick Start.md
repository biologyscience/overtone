# 🧐 How to use ?
After you have started the app, you would get a new window which is the UI of the app.  
Now you can use the appropriate buttons to use the app and play around. If you have identified any bugs, make sure to report them at [Issues](https://github.com/biologyscience/overtone/issues).

## First Launch
- The app will open, asking you to locate the folders you have your songs saved.
- Follow the prompts provided and follow along.
- Once you have selected the folders, the app scans compatible music files and caches the useful metadata (this may take some time at first).
-  *If you don't have any songs with you, head to the [example files](https://github.com/biologyscience/overtone/tree/react/media/songs) that I've shared in this repo and download some.*
-  After the initial scan is complete, the app is pretty straight forward to use. Happy Listening 🥳
